ABOUT

Hello! Thanks for visiting my website! If you don’t know me I’m a local singing artist based in Leeds trying to build sometime meaningful. The project I hope to bring to life has been something that has been in my mind for a long time, its only now I’m growing the courage to share it with everyone…I write songs from a place of vulnerability. I think this is something that can be powerful for people to feel… If you like the sound of RnB, neo-soul, pop with a mix of Chaka Khan, Raye and Amy Winehouse please look around. Right now I have a band and we’ll be performing locally in Leeds/ West Yorkshire region
